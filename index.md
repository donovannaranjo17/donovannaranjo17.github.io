## Featured Project: TrashTalk FM

**From a fantasy football idea to a real web application.**

[Visit TrashTalk FM](https://trashtalkfm.com)

[View Project Repository](https://github.com/donovannaranjo17/fantasy-podcast-platform)

TrashTalk FM is a fantasy football podcast platform that I developed independently. After years of playing fantasy football with friends, I wanted to create something that made the competition, rivalries, and weekly trash talk even more entertaining.

The platform connects to fantasy football services such as Sleeper and Yahoo, analyzes league data, and uses AI to generate personalized podcast episodes based on what happened in each league.

The project has allowed me to gain hands-on experience with Python, FastAPI, JavaScript, API integrations, and deploying and maintaining a real web application.

I'm continuing to improve the platform with new features and ideas.

## CS 499 Capstone Enhancements

For my Computer Science capstone, I'll be improving TrashTalk FM in three areas.

### 1. Software Engineering and Design
**Planned Enhancement: Highlight of the Week Image Generator**

Develop a feature that allows users to generate an AI-created image based on their fantasy league's Highlight of the Week. This will involve frontend development, backend integration, and handling image-generation requests.

### 2. Algorithms and Data Structures
**Planned Enhancement: Weekly Highlight Selection**

Improve how the application evaluates and ranks weekly fantasy football events to identify the most interesting highlights using structured data and scoring algorithms.

### 3. Databases
**Planned Enhancement: Highlight and Image History**

Expand the application's data storage capabilities to organize, save, and retrieve weekly highlights and generated images.
